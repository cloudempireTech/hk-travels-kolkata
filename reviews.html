Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, outstation rules, airport meet-and-greets, and vehicle cleanliness.

How early should I book for CCU Airport pickup?

While we accommodate emergency bookings in 30–45 minutes across South Kolkata, we recommend booking at least 3–4 hours in advance or the previous evening to ensure your preferred vehicle class.

Are toll charges and state taxes included?

All highway tolls are cleared via Fastag for non-stop travel. Chauffeurs maintain digital receipts and duty slips for clear, transparent settlement with zero hidden surcharges.

Can I book for multi-day outstation tours?

Yes! We provide multi-day vehicle retention across Digha, Mandarmani, Purulia, Shantiniketan, Siliguri, and Puri with dedicated chauffeurs accommodated throughout the circuit.
